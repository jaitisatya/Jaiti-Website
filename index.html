Our Purpose

Why Jaiti Foundation Provides Free Education to Underprivileged Children in Jaipur

Jaiti Foundation works alongside schools, communities, volunteers and mentors to include, educate, and empower every child in India's slum communities.

Breaking the Cycle of Poverty in Jaipur

We work to break the intergenerational cycle of poverty through education, giving children tools to create better futures for themselves and their families.

Education is a Right, Not a Privilege

We believe education is not a privilege reserved for the wealthy. Every child, regardless of economic background, deserves access to quality education and opportunities for growth.

Building Complete, Confident Individuals in Sanganer

Our programs go beyond traditional education to create well-rounded individuals equipped with knowledge, values, and life skills for a successful future.

All Our Services are Completely Free

Every service we provide - from education to meals to learning materials - is completely free. No hidden costs, no fees, just pure dedication to helping children succeed.

What We Offer

Free Education Programs for Underprivileged Children in Jaipur

Comprehensive, free services designed to nurture every aspect of a child's development and future success.

Quality Education

Academic excellence should not be limited by economic circumstances. We provide comprehensive, high-quality education completely free of cost, ensuring every child has access to the learning they need to succeed.

  • Complete curriculum coverage
  • Qualified educators
  • Personalized learning approach
  • Regular assessments

Moral Values & Character Building

Education extends beyond academic knowledge. We instill strong moral values, ethics, and character traits that help children become compassionate, responsible individuals who contribute positively to society.

  • Character development programs
  • Ethical decision making
  • Compassion and empathy training
  • Leadership skills

Civic Sense & Social Awareness

Preparing children to be informed, responsible citizens who understand their rights, duties, and role in society. We teach civic awareness, environmental consciousness, and social responsibility.

  • Citizenship education
  • Environmental awareness
  • Community involvement
  • Social responsibility

Nutritious Food Program

Consistent meal provision 365 days a year, ensuring no child goes hungry. Our nutrition program supports healthy physical and cognitive development while allowing children to focus on learning.

  • Daily nutritious meals
  • Year-round food security
  • Healthy growth support
  • Focus on learning
