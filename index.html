Our Purpose

Why Jaiti Foundation Exists

Jaiti Foundation is a child education NGO in Jaipur working with underserved communities to include, educate, and empower every child. Based near Mahal Road in Jagatpura, we serve families from Jagatpura, Pratap Nagar, and nearby areas with free education, moral learning, and holistic support.

Breaking the Cycle

We work to break the intergenerational cycle of poverty through education, giving children tools to create better futures for themselves and their families.

Education is a Right

We believe education is not a privilege reserved for the wealthy, but a fundamental right that every child deserves, regardless of their economic background.

Holistic Development

Beyond academics, we nurture moral values, civic responsibility, and provide nutritious meals, ensuring complete development of each child.

Our Philosophy

Education Changes Everything

At Jaiti Foundation, we don't just teach subjects—we transform lives. Our holistic approach combines academic excellence with character building, ensuring that every child not only learns to read and write, but also develops the values and skills needed to thrive in life.

We provide a safe, nurturing environment where underprivileged children can discover their potential, dream big, and build the confidence to achieve those dreams. Through consistent support, nutritious meals, and caring mentorship, we're creating a generation of educated, empowered, and responsible citizens.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Jaiti Foundation or our programs? Here are answers to the most common questions from our community.

Where is Jaiti Foundation located?

Jaiti Foundation is located on Mahal Road, Jagatpura, Jaipur (Rajasthan 302017), and serves children and families from Jagatpura, Pratap Nagar, Mahal Road, and nearby communities in Jaipur.

What does Jaiti Foundation do?

Jaiti Foundation provides free education, moral learning, civic awareness, nutritious meals, and mentorship for underprivileged children in Jaipur. Our holistic approach ensures every child develops academically, morally, and personally.

Can I volunteer to teach children in Jaipur?

Yes! Jaiti Foundation welcomes volunteers in Jaipur—students, educators, professionals, and community members. You can teach, mentor, conduct workshops, support activities, or help with learning materials. Contact us to get involved.

Which areas does Jaiti Foundation serve?

Jaiti Foundation primarily serves children and families from Jagatpura, Pratap Nagar, Mahal Road, and nearby Jaipur communities. We are based near Mahal Road in Jagatpura and work with underserved neighborhoods in the area.

Does Jaiti Foundation provide free education?

Yes. Jaiti Foundation provides completely free learning support, value education, mentorship, and meals to children from underprivileged communities in Jaipur. All our services are provided at no cost to students and families.

How can I support Jaiti Foundation?

You can support Jaiti Foundation by volunteering your time as an educator or mentor, contributing learning materials, helping with activities, or collaborating on special programs. Reach out to us at jaitifoundation@gmail.com or call +91 6367916384.
